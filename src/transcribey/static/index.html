Transcribey

Turn multi-track or single-speaker audio into an accurate, speaker-labelled, reviewable transcript, entirely on your own computer.

1 Add your recording

Add one audio file for each person. Each file becomes one speaker.

Drop audio files here, or a Craig .zip (no need to unzip)
.flac .wav .mp3 .m4a and most other audio files

2 Make the transcript

Whisper, a speech-to-text model, listens to each person and writes down what they said.
Everything runs on this computer. The model downloads once, the first time you use it.

Pick Thai for Thai talk, also when people mix in English words. Pick English when everyone speaks English. Detect automatically works for other languages but is weaker on mixed speech.
Bigger models are more accurate but slower. Each model downloads once, the first time you use it. The Thai models Typhoon and Pathumma need a one-time install step first; the app shows the command when you pick one.
A graphics card (NVIDIA) is much faster than the processor. If it cannot be used, the app switches to the processor by itself.
Optional: teach it names and terms
A short list of the words that matter helps more than a long one. It is a hint, not a rule: Whisper may still spell some words its own way.

Leave this empty to use the Quality choice above.

To try another model, enter one of these:

  • a size name, e.g. large-v2 or distil-large-v3
  • a faster-whisper repository on Hugging Face, e.g. Systran/faster-whisper-small
  • a model folder on this computer, e.g. the one convert_model prints

A normal Hugging Face Whisper model does not work here. Convert it first with:

python -m transcribey.convert_model <repo>

3 Check and fix

Press ▶ to hear a line and fix any wrong words in its box.
Tick reviewed when a line is right. Changes save automatically.

Keyboard shortcuts and missed speech

Alt+Enter marks the line reviewed and plays the next one. Alt+↓ jumps to the next unsure line or Missed speech row (dashed: someone spoke, no line has the words).

Looks for parts of the audio where someone spoke but no line has their words. Each one shows as a dashed Missed speech row you can listen to, fill in or dismiss.
  • all every line
  • unsure not reviewed, plus Missed speech rows
  • not reviewed not ticked yet
  • TH/EN Thai mixed with English

Unsure: a word is underlined, the text looks made up, or words are missing. Ticking reviewed or editing the line clears it.

4 Save your work

Each line looks like [time] Person: words with everyone merged in time order.
Your work is also kept in the transcribey_work folder, so you can close this page and continue later.

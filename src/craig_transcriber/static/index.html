Craig Transcriber

Turn a Craig recording into a written transcript, then listen line by line and fix the mistakes. Everything stays on this computer.

1 Add your recording

Drop the files you downloaded from Craig. Craig records each person on their own track, so you get one audio file per person, usually inside a .zip.

Drop the .zip or audio files here
.flac, .wav, .mp3 and similar all work

2 Make the transcript

An AI model called Whisper listens to each person and types what they said. It runs on your computer. The first time, the model is downloaded once.

Optional: help it with names and words

Type words that appear in your talk (names, product or technical terms, in the spelling you want). Whisper tends to copy this spelling.

3 Check and fix

Press ▶ on a line to hear it. Type in the box to fix the words. Tick reviewed when a line is right, or press Alt+Enter to tick it and play the next line. Alt+↓ jumps to the next unsure line or Missed speech row, a dashed row where someone spoke but no line holds the words. Edits save automatically.

4 Save your work

Each line is written as [time] Person: words with all people merged in time order. Your work is also saved automatically in the craig_work folder, so you can close this and carry on later.
